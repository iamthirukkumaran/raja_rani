Who this is from

Raja Rani ("the app", "we", "us") is a free multiplayer party card game for 4–5 players on Android and iOS. One player creates a room, the others join with a six-character code, and every phone is dealt a secret role.

This policy explains what the app and this site collect, why, who can see it, and how to get rid of it. Questions and requests go to kumaranthiru478@gmail.com.

What the app collects

The game has no sign-up, so there is no profile to fill in. Everything below is either typed by you in one field, produced by playing a round, or needed to keep the phones at one table in sync.

What you type

  • Display name. Shown to the other players in your room, and used to draw your initials avatar. A nickname is fine, and you can change it any time in Settings.
  • Room setup. If you create the room: its name, the player count and the number of rounds.

What playing produces

  • Room and round data. The room code, who is seated, which role each phone was dealt, the Police's guess and the caught-or-escaped verdict. This is the game — every phone in the room needs it to stay on the same round.
  • Your stats and history. Games played, wins and points, plus a record of past games, so the Stats and History screens have something to show you.

What stays on your phone only

  • Settings and session. Your sound and vibration toggles, your name, and enough session detail to show the rejoin banner and put you back in your seat if the app closes mid-game. This lives in the app's own storage and goes when you uninstall.

Technical data

  • A player ID. A random identifier the app generates so our servers can tell one phone from another and reconnect you to your seat. It is not your advertising ID, and it is not linked to a name, email or phone number anywhere outside the game.
  • Connection data. While you are connected, your device's IP address is visible to our servers, as it is to any server you talk to. We use it to deliver the game and to block abuse — not to locate you.
  • Crash and performance reports. If the app crashes we may receive a diagnostic report: device model, OS and app version, and a technical trace of what the app was doing. We use it to fix bugs.
  • Store statistics. Google Play and the App Store give us aggregate install, rating and crash figures under their own privacy policies. We cannot tie those to an individual player.

This website

The page you are reading is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no tracking pixels. Fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives the request as part of delivering them. GitHub keeps standard server logs of requests under its own privacy statement.

What we never collect

  • No email address, password or phone number — there is nothing to log in to.
  • No location, coarse or precise. The app never asks.
  • No contacts, calendar, photos, files or microphone.
  • No advertising identifier, and no SDK that follows you across other apps or websites.
  • No payment details. The game is free and sells nothing inside it.
  • No health, biometric, financial or government-ID data.
  • No voice or text chat content — the app has neither. You talk to each other in the room.

Permissions the app asks for

Three, and two of them are optional.

PermissionWhen it's askedWhat it's for

Why we use it

  • To run a room and keep every phone on the same round.
  • To show your name and initials to the others at the table.
  • To score rounds and keep your stats, history and the final podium.
  • To put you back in your seat after a disconnect.
  • To find and fix crashes, and keep the servers stable.
  • To prevent abuse of the service.

If you are in the EEA or the UK: we rely on performance of a contract for everything needed to run the game you asked to play, and on our legitimate interests for security, abuse prevention and debugging. We do not process your data for advertising, so we never ask you to consent to it.

What other players see

Inside a room, the other players see your display name, your initials avatar, whether your seat is filled, your score, and — once a round ends and the verdict is in — the role you were dealt. While the round is live your card stays private to your own phone.

A room code is a key: anyone who has it can join that room until it fills or closes. Share it only with the people you want at the table, and pick a display name you are happy for them to see.

Who else sees it

  • Nobody, for money. We do not sell, rent or trade your data, and we do not share it for advertising or cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • Infrastructure providers. The game servers, database and crash reporting run on third-party cloud infrastructure. Those providers process data only on our instructions and only to keep the service running.
  • The app stores. Apple and Google distribute the app and report aggregate statistics to us, under their own privacy policies.
  • The law. If we are legally required to disclose something — a valid court order, for example — we will, and we will tell affected players where we are permitted to.

How long we keep it

  • Live room data lasts as long as the room does. Once a room closes and its results are written to the players' histories, the room itself is cleared.
  • Your name, stats and history stay until you delete them, or until the player ID has been inactive for 24 months, whichever comes first.
  • Crash reports are kept only as long as it takes to fix the bug, and no longer than 90 days.
  • On-device data goes the moment you uninstall the app.

Deleting your data

There are two routes, and the first one is instant and needs no email from you.

In the app: Settings → Delete my data → confirm.

That removes your display name, initials, stats, full game history and your player ID from our servers, and clears the app's storage on your phone. It cannot be undone, and we do not keep a shadow copy.

If you can't reach the button — you have lost or wiped the device, say — email kumaranthiru478@gmail.com with the display name you played under and roughly when you played. Because the game has no login, there is no account for us to verify you against, so we can only act on what we can confidently match. We answer within 7 days and complete deletions within 30 days, including copies held in backups.

Uninstalling the app on its own removes everything stored on the phone, but not the records on our servers — use the button or write to us for those.

Security

  • Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit with TLS.
  • Access to the servers and database is limited to the people who maintain the game.
  • The strongest protection is not holding things in the first place, which is why there is no login, no email on file and no advertising ID.

No service can promise perfect security. If a breach ever affects your data, we will notify affected players and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

Children

Raja Rani is a family-friendly party game, but it is not directed at children under 13 — or under the minimum age where you live, if that is higher — and we do not knowingly collect data from them. The app has no chat, no purchases and no ads.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us data, write to kumaranthiru478@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Your rights

Most of these you can exercise yourself, without asking us: your name is editable in Settings, your history is visible in the app, and Delete my data erases the lot. For anything else, write to kumaranthiru478@gmail.com.

  • India (DPDP Act, 2023). You have the right to access a summary of your data, to have it corrected or completed, to have it erased, and to raise a grievance. The contact address above is our grievance channel; if we do not resolve it, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.
  • EEA and UK (GDPR). You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and you may complain to your local supervisory authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA). You have the right to know, delete and correct, and not to be discriminated against for exercising them. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Using any of these rights never costs you anything or changes how the game plays.

Where it's handled

We are based in India, and the servers that run the game may sit in a different country from the one you play in. Where data moves across borders, we rely on our providers' standard contractual clauses and equivalent safeguards, and we keep the amount of data that moves as small as the game allows.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the last updated date at the top changes with it. If a change materially affects what we collect or what we do with it, we will say so in the app before it takes effect — not quietly, in a diff.

Contact

Privacy questions, data requests and grievances: kumaranthiru478@gmail.com. We reply within 7 days, and resolve requests within 30.
