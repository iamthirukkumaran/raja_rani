The game

No paper. No arguments.
No sign-up.

Raja Rani brings the childhood paper-chit game to phones. Everyone opens the app, joins one room and gets a secret role card. The Police has to find the Thief. The app deals the cards, decides the outcome and keeps every score.

  • One code gets the whole group in.
  • Your card is yours alone — nobody else can see it.
  • The app calls it, so nobody argues about who pointed first.

The characters

Five cards. One of them is lying.

The Raja and the Police are announced to everyone. The other cards stay secret — tap a card to turn it over.

Each role lands with its own moment — a crown drops, petals fall, a siren flashes, smoke puffs.

How a round works

Shuffle, reveal, hunt,
verdict, score.

  1. 1

    Shuffle

    The player whose turn it is taps the deck. Cards spin on every phone at once.

  2. 2

    Reveal

    Each player taps to flip their own secret card in 3D. Only you see yours.

  3. 3

    Hunt

    The Police sees every suspect except the Raja, taps one, then taps CATCH!

  4. 4

    Verdict

    The app checks the deal and stamps CAUGHT or ESCAPED. No arguing.

  5. 5

    Score

    Points count up and the shuffle passes to the next player.

The scoring table

The host chooses 5, 10, 15 or 20 rounds. After the last round, the highest total wins. Only the Police and the Thief have anything at stake.

The last round

Crown the winner.

A podium rises for 1st, 2nd and 3rd, a crown drops on the winner, and fireworks and confetti fill the screen. The host can start a rematch in the same room, and every finished game is saved to each player's history and stats.
